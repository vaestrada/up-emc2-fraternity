# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences, in order: (1) alumni brods (1967–2023 batches, many now senior engineers and executives) checking in on the brotherhood, considering donations, updating records; (2) the UP Engineering community — students, faculty, partner organizations — forming an impression of the fraternity; (3) the Alumni Association board using the site as the official public record. Most visits are evaluative, not task-driven: people arrive to feel whether the institution is credible, alive, and worth their pride or money.

## Product Purpose

The first official web presence of the EMC² Fraternity (est. 1969, U.P. College of Engineering) and its SEC-registered Alumni Association. It must read as the digital equivalent of a hall of records: history, projects, honored members, donation channels, correspondence. Success: an alumnus lands here and feels the 57-year weight of the institution within five seconds, then finds the give-back or contact path without friction.

## Brand Personality

Engraved, learned, quietly powerful. Three words: monumental, precise, fraternal. The voice of a state hall or a university charter, not a startup or an event page. Motto: "Engineered for Service." Secondary motif: quantum-leap wordplay on the E=mc² name (marquee, subtitle), kept witty but never cute.

## Anti-references

- Festive/glitter atmospheres (bokeh particles, sparkle videos): the site must never read as "Christmas" (explicit owner feedback).
- Generic student-org sites: clip-art crests, centered Word-document layouts, rainbow Facebook embeds.
- SaaS landing-page tropes: gradient text, glass cards, hero-metric blocks, identical icon-card grids.
- Anything that feels rented: stock photos of strangers, template themes.

## Design Principles

1. **Permanence over spectacle.** Every choice should look correct in ten years. Motion is few, slow, decisive (ceremonial restraint); stillness is the default state.
2. **The wordmark is the monument.** Typography carries the hero and section heads; imagery and ornament serve it, never compete.
3. **Engineering is the ornament.** Drafting grids, schematic annotations, figure captions, coordinates: decoration derives from the discipline, not from fashion.
4. **The archive metaphor governs.** Sections are numbered entries (№ 01…); photos are treated as archival plates; the ledger, citations, and patronage language stays consistent.
5. **Gold is scarce.** Gold marks honor and action (foil heads, CTAs, hairlines). It never becomes wallpaper.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Full prefers-reduced-motion support (CSS and framer-motion already wired). Aging alumni audience: body text never below 14px effective, strong contrast on the dark ground, generous hit targets. No information conveyed by motion or color alone.
